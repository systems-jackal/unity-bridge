Meet the Team

We don’t just build software – we build a culture of connection. Our team is structured exactly the way we design systems: with clear ownership, seamless collaboration, and an obsession with eliminating fragmentation.

Dylan Kibet

Technical Lead / Backend & Infrastructure

System architecture · Backend (Java/Rust) · DevOps · Security

Dylan drives the technical direction of Unity Bridge. He owns the system architecture, backend services, infrastructure, and engineering standards. By designing scalable, secure systems from the ground up, he ensures that no part of the technology stack becomes a silo. All critical technical decisions – from API design to deployment pipelines – go through him, guaranteeing that the foundation of every ecosystem is solid and connected.

Bernard Korir

Product & Requirements Engineer

Requirements · User stories · Backlog · Product logic

Bernard defines what we build. He translates ideas into crystal‑clear user stories and acceptance criteria, working closely with Dylan to validate technical feasibility. His clarity prevents misalignment and rework – the very fragmentation that kills projects. By managing the product backlog and leading sprint planning, he ensures the team always builds the right thing, with no ambiguity.

Ezra Tomno

Frontend Engineer & Quality Assurance Lead

Frontend · API integration · Testing · Quality

Ezra builds the user‑facing part of the system and leads quality assurance. He implements responsive, accessible interfaces based on Bernard’s requirements and ensures they integrate seamlessly with Dylan’s APIs. Through rigorous testing (unit, integration, end‑to‑end) and meticulous bug tracking, he verifies that every piece of the ecosystem works together. He has the authority to block releases that don’t meet quality standards – because a connected system is only as strong as its weakest link.
