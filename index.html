OUR STORY

I saw the same problem over and over: nothing was connected.

My name is Dylan Kibet. I’m a computer security and forensics student. While studying, I watched small businesses spend thousands on a website, then a booking app, then a POS from another vendor, and finally hire someone to “make it all work.” The result was always the same – data silos, security gaps, and frustrated owners.

I realised the root cause wasn’t a lack of tools. It was that no one was thinking about how those tools fit together. A beautiful website that doesn’t drive sales. A booking system that doesn’t feed into the calendar. Security added as an afterthought. That’s a fragmented system – and it’s costing you time, money, and peace of mind.

So I started Unity Bridge. Instead of handing you another isolated tool, I design connected digital ecosystems – where your website, bookings, inventory, and security work as one seamless, secure engine. No fragmentation. No extra effort. Just technology that actually grows your business.
